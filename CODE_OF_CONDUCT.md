# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss technical decisions without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Respect maintainers' time: provide clear reproduction steps, keep discussions relevant, and avoid duplicate reports. Follow the security policy when reporting vulnerabilities.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an available contact channel. Do not post private reports or personal details in public issues.
